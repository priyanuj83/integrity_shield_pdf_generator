\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item A student hypothesizes that the amount of water a plant receives will affect how long it grows before reaching maturity. To test this hypothesis, the student...
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item several digital scales.
    \item a wet paper towel.
    \item several identical plant seeds.
    \item different sizes of containers.
\end{enumerate}
\item Find the opposite of the number -7.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 7
    \item -7
    \item 0.7
    \item -7.7

\item In MSM, early syphilis was found to be associated with:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item HIV infection
    \item Use of methamphetamine
    \item Sex with recent internet partners

\item An appliance manufacturer has redesigned its original electrical fan. It has made the new fan more energy efficient. This means that the new fan
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item converts more electricity into heat.
    \item spins at a slower rate than the original fan.
    \item requires more electricity than the original fan.
    \item reduces the percentage of heat lost to the atmosphere.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following processes is responsible for changing liquid water into water vapor?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item photosynthesis
    \item condensation
    \item evaporation
    \item precipitation
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

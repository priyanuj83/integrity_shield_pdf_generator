\section*{Multiple Choice (20 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following is an example of genetic engineering?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Growing a whole plant from a single cell.
    \item Finding the sequences of bases in plant DNA.
    \item Inserting a gene into plants that makes them resistant to insects.
    \item Attaching the root of one type of plant to the stem of another type of plant.
\end{enumerate}
\item Hume argues that the criteria for judging aesthetic value are \_\_\_\_\_.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item entirely subjective
    \item unchangeable
    \item entirely objective
    \item unknown
\end{enumerate}
\item SWOT is an acronym for:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats.
    \item Strategy, work, openness, toughness.
    \item Strategy, weakness, opinions, tactics.
    \item Strategy, working, opinion, tactical.
\end{enumerate}
\item A person is 6 feet tall. Which of the following metric measurements is most near to 6 feet?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 6 meters
    \item 3 meters
    \item 2 meters
    \item 1 meter
\end{enumerate}
\item Dopamine is prescribed at a rate of 4 micrograms/kg/min. For a 65 kg person, how many milligrams will they receive in an hour?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 156
    \item 15.6
    \item 1.56
    \item 15600
\end{enumerate}
\item Arguing that someone should do something because other people are doing it is a description of which of the following fallacies?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item appeal to common practice
    \item snob appeal
    \item argument by consensus
    \item appeal to tradition
\end{enumerate}
\item Which features are physical properties of an iron nail?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item conducts heat, magnetic, solid
    \item conducts electricity, magnetic, rusts
    \item conducts sound, insulates, non-magnetic
    \item dissolves, conducts heat, non-magnetic
\end{enumerate}
\item A student uses clay to build models of an ocean plate and a continental plate. What characteristic cannot be accurately represented by the models?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the shapes of plates
    \item the relative sizes of plates
    \item the relative densities of plates
    \item the arrangement of plates relative to one another
\end{enumerate}
\item If people expect the price of a particular product to increase in the near future
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item this will not affect the demand for the product right now.
    \item this will decrease the demand for the product.
    \item this will not affect the demand for the product now or later.
    \item this will increase the demand for the product.
\end{enumerate}
\item In the assessment of the hand function which of the following is true?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Abduction of the thumb is supplied by spinal root T2
    \item Opposition of the thumb by opponens policis is supplied by spinal root T1
    \item Finger adduction is supplied by the median nerve
    \item Finger abduction is mediated by the palmar interossei
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

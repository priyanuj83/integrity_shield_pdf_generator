\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item All of the following statements about gerrymandering are true EXCEPT that it
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

    \item can divide like-minded voters into several districts to reduce their influence

\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Armed conflict

    \item II and III
    \item I, II, and III
    \item I, II, III, and IV
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the Powell Doctrine.
    \item the Roosevelt Doctrine.
    \item the Bush Doctrine.
    \item the Monroe Doctrine.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following did not emerge as a trend of the defence trade in the 20 th century?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

    \item An increase in the defence trade.
    \item A decrease in the arms trade.
    \item A growth in the number and variety of weapons traded.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

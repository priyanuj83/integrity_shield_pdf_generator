\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item A member of which of the following demographic groups is most likely to support a Republican presidential candidate?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Married white male
    \item Unmarried white female
    \item African American, male or female
    \item Youths under the age of 25, male or female
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is a legitimate threat to societal security?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Political policies designed to alter demographic characteristics of a state.
    \item All of these options.
    \item Legislation that criminalises certain cultural behaviours or practice.
    \item The use of military force to conduct ethnic-cleansing through displacement and killing.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is an example of "horse-race journalism"?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A news story focuses on a politician's scandals rather than achievements.
    \item A television news anchor reports an event before the station's rivals.
    \item A reporter announces which candidate leads in a public opinion poll.
    \item A newspaper editor prints stories about long-term political developments.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following has the Supreme Court held about the death penalty?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The death penalty may only be imposed upon citizens.
    \item Under some circumstances, the death penalty may not violate the Eighth Amendment.
    \item Under some circumstances, the death penalty may violate the Third Amendment.
    \item States may execute any adult regardless of intellectual disability.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following points supports the argument for American unilateralism?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Benevolent hegemony
    \item Ineffectiveness of multilateralism
    \item American power
    \item All of the above
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

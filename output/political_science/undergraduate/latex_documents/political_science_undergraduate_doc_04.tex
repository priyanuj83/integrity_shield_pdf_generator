\item What was unique about the NATO intervention in Kosovo?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

    \item administer elections
    \item impose taxes
    \item establish courts
    \item borrow money
\end{enumerate}
\item The Supreme Court has used the practice of selective incorporation to
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item limit the number of appeals filed by defendants in state courts
    \item extend voting rights to racial minorities and women
    \item apply most Bill of Rights protections to state law
    \item hasten the integration of public schools
\end{enumerate}
\item The difference between a pardon and a reprieve is

    \item a pardon grants a release from legal punishment while a reprieve postpones it
    \item only state governors can issue pardons
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

Deterrence theory built upon these ideas, focusing on how states could prevent aggression through the credible threat of retaliation, which led to the notion of 'extended deterrence.' Together, these theories created a reality where the calculations of power dynamics and security were abstracted into formalized strategies, informing both policy-making and academic discourse on international relations.

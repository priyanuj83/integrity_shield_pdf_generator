\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item What was the key difference between US expansion pre- and post- 1865?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item US expansion was based on territory rather than markets post-1865
    \item US expansion was based on markets rather than territory post-1865
    \item US expansion was limited to Latin America post-1865
    \item US expansion ended after 1865
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is a legitimate threat to societal security?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Political policies designed to alter demographic characteristics of a state.
    \item All of these options.
    \item Legislation that criminalises certain cultural behaviours or practice.
    \item The use of military force to conduct ethnic-cleansing through displacement and killing.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is not a motivation for utilising aid as an economic policy for security?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item To reward recipients for the cessation of negative behaviour, such as human rights abuses.
    \item To incentivise recipients to act in ways beneficial to the donor.
    \item Compassion for the human suffering of others.
    \item To influence recipients or potential recipients through granting or denying aid.
\end{enumerate}
\item In American government, the power to declare war rests with
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the president of the United States.
    \item the secretary of defense.
    \item the chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff.
    \item Congress.
\end{enumerate}
\item When considering economic tools for security policy, which of the following is the odd one out?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Withdrawal of economic trade rights with the domestic market.
    \item Export controls protecting technological advantage and further foreign policy objectives.
    \item Control of munitions and arms sales.
    \item Import restrictions to protect a domestic market from foreign goods.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

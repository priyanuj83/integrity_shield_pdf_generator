\section*{Multiple Choice (20 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following is not correct about economies of scale?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Economies of scale are associated with increases in production of output.
    \item Economies of scale are associated with the rising or increasing portion of an average total cost (ATC) curve.
    \item Economies of scale are associated with the declining or decreasing portions of the ATC curve.
    \item Economies of scale result in decreases in per unit average cost.
\end{enumerate}
\item If the FED buys bonds in the secondary market
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the money supply will increase.
    \item the money supply will decrease.
    \item the money supply will not be affected.
    \item the discount rate would be affected.
\end{enumerate}
\item When a perfectly competitive labor market is in equilibrium,
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item everyone who wants to work has the opportunity to do so
    \item individual firms face downward sloping labor demand curves
    \item unemployment can reach as high as 10-15 percent
    \item individual firms face upward sloping labor demand curves
\end{enumerate}
\item The theory of rational expectations
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item assumes that consumers and businesses anticipate rising prices when the government pursues an expansionary fiscal policy.
    \item implies that fiscal policy will be effective even during stagflation.
    \item supports the notion of a Phillips tradeoff.
    \item assumes that consumers and businesses do not use all the information available to them.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which choice produces a faster rate of economic growth for the United States?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Institution of higher tariffs on imported goods
    \item More investment in capital infrastructure and less consumption of nondurable goods and services
    \item Elimination of mandatory school attendance laws
    \item Annual limits on the number of foreigners immigrating into the United States
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following indicates that two goods are complements?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A positive income elasticity
    \item A horizontal demand curve
    \item A negative cross-price elasticity
    \item A demand elasticity greater than one
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following will cause the aggregate demand curve to shift to the left?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Expectations of surpluses of goods in the future
    \item A decrease in income taxes
    \item An increase in government spending
    \item An increase in foreign income
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following best measures changes in the price level of national product?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The consumer price index
    \item The real interest rate
    \item The unemployment rate
    \item The GDP deflator
\end{enumerate}
\item Business cycles
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item occur infrequently in capitalist economies.
    \item refer to reusing resources in production.
    \item are predictable ups and downs in economic activity.
    \item are each comprised of a recession and an expansion.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is true?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A marginal social cost = marginal private cost + marginal social benefits.
    \item Value of externality = marginal social costs.
    \item Value of externality = marginal private costs.
    \item Marginal social cost = marginal private cost + negative externality.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

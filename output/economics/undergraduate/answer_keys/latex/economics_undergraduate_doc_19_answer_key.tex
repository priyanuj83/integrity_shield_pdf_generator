In this situation, the firm is unable to cover its variable costs, leading to greater losses than if it were to cease production altogether. Additionally, if the price is less than the minimum average total cost (ATC), the firm will not be able to sustain its operations in the long run, as it will not earn sufficient revenue to cover its total costs.

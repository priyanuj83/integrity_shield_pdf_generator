In this case, the transaction is a transfer of ownership of an existing asset, which does not reflect new production or economic activity. While the sale may involve a price that reflects the car's market value, it does not generate new output or income that would be counted in GDP calculations. Additionally, certain transactions, such as the sale of used goods, are excluded from GDP to avoid double counting, as these goods have already been accounted for in previous production figures. \\ \textit{Note:} correct because GDP only accounts for the value of newly produced goods and services, and the sale of a used car represents a transfer of ownership rather than new economic production.

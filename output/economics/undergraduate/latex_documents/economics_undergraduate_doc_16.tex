\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following would reduce economic growth?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item An increase in immigration from abroad.
    \item A rise in the level of education.
    \item A rise in the literacy rate.
    \item A decrease in unemployment rates.
    \item A growth in the number of small businesses.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following increases the size of the tax multiplier?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item An increase in the reserve ratio.
    \item An increase in the money supply.
    \item A decrease in the marginal propensity to consume.
    \item A decrease in the spending multiplier.
    \item An increase in the interest rate.
\end{enumerate}
\item Assume the reserve requirement is 10 percent. If the FED sells \$29 million worth of government securities in an open market operation then the money supply can
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item decrease by \$29 million.
    \item increase by \$2900 million.
    \item decrease by \$2.9 million.
    \item stay the same.
    \item increase by \$29 million.
\end{enumerate}
\item Tariffs and quotas
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item affect domestic prices: the former lowers them while the latter raises them.
    \item reduce the overall cost of goods for consumers.
    \item have no impact on the volume of trade between nations.
    \item result in higher domestic prices.
    \item ensure the stability of international markets without influencing domestic markets.
\end{enumerate}
\item Consider the following model for $y_t$: $y_t = \mu + \lambda t + u_t$ Which one of the following most accurately describes the process for $y_t$?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A non-stationary process without trend
    \item A random walk with drift
    \item A stationary process
    \item A mean-reverting process
    \item A stationary process with trend
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following policies best describes supply-side fiscal policy?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Higher taxes on household income
    \item Increased government spending
    \item Increased taxes on corporate profits
    \item Increased import tariffs
    \item Decreased interest rates
\end{enumerate}
\item The Law of Diminishing Marginal Returns is responsible for
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item MC that first falls, but eventually rises, as output increases.
    \item AP that first falls, but eventually rises, as output increases.
    \item TC that first falls, but eventually rises, as output increases.
    \item AVC that first rises, but eventually falls, as output increases.
    \item MP that first falls, but eventually rises, as output increases.
\end{enumerate}
\item When both short-run aggregate supply and aggregate demand increase which of the following can be said for certain?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Both the price level and real GDP fall.
    \item The price level rises but the change in real GDP is uncertain.
    \item The price level falls but the change in real GDP is uncertain.
    \item Real GDP falls but the change in the price level is uncertain.
    \item The price level rises but real GDP falls.
\end{enumerate}
\item If the U.S. dollar and Chinese yuan are traded in flexible currency markets which of the following causes an appreciation of the dollar relative to the Chinese yuan?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Rising per capita GDP in China increasing imports from the United States
    \item Lower per capita GDP in China decreasing imports from the United States
    \item Rising unemployment rates in the United States
    \item Higher price levels in the United States relative to China
    \item Decrease in the American GDP
\end{enumerate}
\item What do we mean when we say that the farm problem may be cor-rectly envisioned as a problem of resource misallocation?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Overproduction and wastage of crops
    \item Lack of alternative employment
    \item Excessive government intervention in agriculture
    \item Inefficient farming methods
    \item Low income of farmers
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item A minimum wage in the market for fast-food workers is likely to produce
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item an increase in the demand for fast-food workers.
    \item a decrease in the supply of fast-food workers.
    \item a shortage of fast-food workers.
    \item a surplus of fast-food workers.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is not a scarce economic resource?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Labor
    \item Capital
    \item Human wants
    \item Land
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is correct about the demand for labor?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The demand for labor is independent of the demand for other inputs or resources.
    \item The demand for labor is independent of the demand for the products produced by labor.
    \item The demand for labor is independent of the availability of other inputs or resources.
    \item The demand for labor is derived from the demand for the products produced by labor.
\end{enumerate}
\item If interest rates rise relatively more in country A than in country B then the value of country A's currency will
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item appreciate.
    \item depreciate.
    \item remain unchanged.
    \item change indeterminately.
\end{enumerate}
\item The market demand curve for labor will shift to the right when
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the number of firms increases
    \item the price of output decreases
    \item the labor supply curve shifts to the right
    \item the labor supply curve shifts to the left
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

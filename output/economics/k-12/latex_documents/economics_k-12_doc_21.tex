\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following might worsen a nation's trade deficit?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Lower wages relative to other nations.
    \item Lower taxes on corporate profits relative to other nations.
    \item A higher interest rate on financial assets relative to other nations.
    \item A higher rate of inflation relative to other nations.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following goods is likely to have the most elastic demand curve?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Demand for white Ford minivans
    \item Demand for automobiles
    \item Demand for Ford automobiles
    \item Demand for American-made automobiles
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following transactions would be counted in GDP?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The cash you receive from babysitting your neighbor's kids
    \item The sale of illegal drugs
    \item The sale of cucumbers to a pickle manufacturer
    \item The sale of a pound of tomatoes at a supermarket
\end{enumerate}
\item Holding all else equal which of the following monetary policies would be used to boost U.S. exports?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Increasing the discount rate
    \item Increasing the reserve ratio
    \item Buying government securities
    \item Lowering tariffs
\end{enumerate}
\item If interest rates rise in the United States relative to other nations then
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the value of the dollar will tend to appreciate.
    \item the value of the dollar will tend to depreciate.
    \item exchange rates will be affected but not the value of the dollar.
    \item the exchange rate will not be affected.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following causes the supply curve of paper to shift to the left?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Paper producers expect lower paper prices in the months ahead.
    \item The price of pencils, a complement to paper, increases.
    \item Improvements in the technology used to produce paper.
    \item Environmental concerns reduce the yearly amount of timber that can be harvested.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following statements is true?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The velocity of money is equal to real GDP divided by the money supply.
    \item Dollars earned today have more purchasing power than dollars earned a year from today.
    \item The supply of loanable funds consists of investors.
    \item The demand for loanable funds consists of savers.
\end{enumerate}
\item The total utility from sardines is maximized when they are purchased until
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item marginal utility is zero
    \item marginal benefit equals marginal cost
    \item consumer surplus is zero
    \item distributive efficiency is achieved
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following insures the value of the U.S. dollar?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The euro and other foreign currencies held by the Federal Reserve
    \item Gold bars in secure locations like Fort Knox
    \item The promise of the U.S. government to maintain its value
    \item The value of the actual paper on which it is printed.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is more likely to result from a competitive market structure than from a monopoly making the same product?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Price equal to marginal cost
    \item Relative welfare loss
    \item Relatively high price
    \item Relatively low quantity
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Inflation
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item hurts creditors who do not anticipate it.
    \item hurts creditors who anticipate it.
    \item hurts debtors.
    \item both A and D are correct.
\end{enumerate}
\item If the government subsidizes the production of halogen headlights,
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the demand curve will shift to the left.
    \item the demand curve will shift to the right.

    \item the supply curve will shift to the right.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item frictionally unemployed.
    \item cyclically unemployed.
    \item seasonally unemployed.
    \item structurally unemployed.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item marginal cost (MC) intersects AVC and ATC at their maximum points.
    \item AVC and ATC intersect MC at its maximum point.
    \item MC intersects AVC and ATC at their minimum points.
    \item AVC and ATC intersect MC at its minimum point.
\end{enumerate}
\item Fiscal policy refers to
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item increases in taxes to fight recessions.
    \item decreases in taxes to fight inflations.
    \item changes in government spending and taxes to fight recessions or inflations.
    \item federal deficits.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

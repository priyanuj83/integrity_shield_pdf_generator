\item Which of the following will cause the aggregate demand curve to shift to the left?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Expectations of surpluses of goods in the future
    \item A decrease in income taxes
    \item An increase in government spending
    \item An increase in foreign income

\item Total spending
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item is all of the spending done by all of the households in a nation.
    \item slopes downward because less spending occurs when prices rise.
    \item slopes downward because less spending occurs when income falls.
    \item slopes upward because more household spending occurs when income rises.
\end{enumerate}
\item If Nation A can produce a good at lower opportunity cost than Nation B can produce the same good it is said that
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Nation A has comparative advantage in the production of that good.
    \item Nation B has comparative advantage in the production of that good.
    \item Nation A has absolute advantage in the production of that good.
    \item Nation B has absolute advantage in the production of that good.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is most likely to produce stronger economic growth over time?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

    \item A falling stock of capital goods.
    \item Investment tax credits.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

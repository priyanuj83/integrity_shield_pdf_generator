\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Country A has a current account surplus; this means that
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Country A also has a financial account surplus.
    \item Country A has a financial account deficit.
    \item Country A must borrow from its foreign reserves.
    \item Country A is operating in a recessionary gap.
\end{enumerate}
\item If a firm decreases its prices by 15 percent and its total revenue increases by 30 percent, which of the following is correct?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The price elasticity of demand is unit elastic.
    \item The price elasticity of demand is inelastic.
    \item The price elasticity of demand is elastic.
    \item The numerical coefficient of elasticity is equal to one.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following will shift the demand curve for the Mexican peso to the left?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Speculation that the peso will increase in value
    \item An increase in interest rates in Mexico relative to other countries
    \item A lower rate of inflation in Mexico relative to other countries
    \item A decrease in the international demand for Mexican-made textiles
\end{enumerate}
\item Suppose the reserve ratio is 0.1. If a bank gets \$200 in deposits what is the maximum amount it can lend?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item \$20
    \item \$180
    \item \$2,000
    \item Greater than $200 but less than $500
\end{enumerate}
\item The Law of Diminishing Marginal Returns is responsible for
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item AVC that first rises, but eventually falls, as output increases.
    \item AFC that first rises, but eventually falls, as output increases.
    \item MP that first falls, but eventually rises, as output increases.
    \item MC that first falls, but eventually rises, as output increases.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following is a consequence of a protective tariff on imported steel?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Net exports fall.
    \item Income is transferred from domestic steel consumers to domestic steel producers.
    \item Allocative efficiency is improved.
    \item Income is transferred from domestic steel to foreign steel producers.
\end{enumerate}
\item Corn is exchanged in a competitive market. Which of the following definitely increases the equilibrium price of corn?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Both supply and demand shift rightward.
    \item Both supply and demand shift leftward.
    \item Supply shifts to the right; demand shifts to the left.
    \item Supply shifts to the left; demand shifts to the right.
\end{enumerate}
\item If prices rise in the United States relative to other countries then
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the value of the dollar will tend to appreciate.
    \item the value of the dollar will tend to depreciate.
    \item exchange rates will be affected but not the value of the dollar.
    \item the exchange rate will not be affected.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following statements is accurate in regard to a perfectly competitive firm?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Demand is downward-sloping.
    \item The demand curve lies above the marginal revenue curve.
    \item Price is determined by the equilibrium in the entire market.
    \item Average revenue differs from price.
\end{enumerate}
\item If corn is produced in a perfectly competitive market and the government placed a price ceiling above equilibrium, which of the following would be true?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item There would be no change in the amount of corn demanded or supplied.
    \item There would be a shortage created of corn.
    \item There would be a surplus created of corn.
    \item The producers of corn would lose revenue due to the decreased price.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

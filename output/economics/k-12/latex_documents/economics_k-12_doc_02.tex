\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item In the United States over the years V and Q
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item have not changed significantly.
    \item are perfectly constant.
    \item have decreased significantly.
    \item have increased significantly.
\end{enumerate}
\item For which of the following statements are both the price change and quantity change determinable (for a particular good)?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Both the supply of and the demand for the good decrease.
    \item Both the supply of and the demand for the good increase.
    \item The supply increases and the demand for the good decreases.
    \item None of the above.
\end{enumerate}
\item The money supply will increase as a result of which of the following?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A decrease in the required reserve ratio
    \item An increase in the discount rate
    \item The selling of bonds by the Federal Reserve
    \item An increase in the fraction of deposits that must be held by banks
\end{enumerate}
\item When both short-run aggregate supply and aggregate demand increase which of the following can be said for certain?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The price level rises but real GDP falls.
    \item Both the price level and real GDP rise.
    \item The price level rises but the change in real GDP is uncertain.
    \item Real GDP rises but the change in the price level is uncertain.
\end{enumerate}
\item A consequence of a price floor is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item a persistent shortage of the good.
    \item an increase in total welfare.
    \item a persistent surplus of the good.
    \item elimination of deadweight loss.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

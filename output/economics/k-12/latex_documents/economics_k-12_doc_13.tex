\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Stagflation occurs when
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the price level rises for two consecutive quarters
    \item the price level rises and output falls
    \item the price level stays the same and output increases
    \item the price level stays the same and output decreases
\end{enumerate}
\item Suppose that there are only two goods: x and y. Which of the following is NOT correct?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item One can have comparative advantage in producing both goods.
    \item One can have both an absolute advantage and a comparative advantage in producing x.
    \item One can have absolute advantage and no comparative advantage in producing x.
    \item One can have comparative advantage and no absolute advantage in producing x.
\end{enumerate}
\item A fixed output level in the long run at full-employment output corresponds with which of the following shapes of the long-run Phillips curve?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Horizontal
    \item Convex to the origin
    \item Concave to the origin
    \item Vertical
\end{enumerate}
\item Which characteristic is likely a part of a monopoly market but not of monopolistic competition?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Differentiated products
    \item Patents and copyrights
    \item Possibility of profit in the short run
    \item Deadweight loss exists
\end{enumerate}
\item Suppose a price floor is installed in the market for coffee. One result of this policy would be
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item a decrease in the demand for coffee-brewing machines.
    \item a persistent shortage of coffee in the market.
    \item an increase in consumer surplus due to lower coffee prices.
    \item an increase in the demand for coffee.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

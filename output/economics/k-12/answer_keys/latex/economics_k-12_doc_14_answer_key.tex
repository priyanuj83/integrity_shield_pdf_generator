\item \textbf{Answer:} Expansionary monetary policy is a tool used by central banks to stimulate the economy during a recession. By lowering interest rates and increasing the money supply, it encourages businesses and individuals to invest more. When businesses invest, they create new projects and expand operations, which boosts aggregate demand-the total demand for goods and services in the economy. As aggregate demand increases, companies need to hire more workers to meet this demand, leading to higher employment levels. Overall, expansionary monetary policy helps to revive economic activity by promoting investment, increasing aggregate demand, and ultimately reducing unemployment.
\\ \textit{Note:} correct because it accurately describes how expansionary monetary policy lowers interest rates and increases the money supply to stimulate investment, which in turn raises aggregate demand and promotes higher employment levels during a recession.
\item \textbf{Answer:} A rational consumer deciding how many Girl Scout cookies to eat will consider the concept of marginal benefit and marginal cost. The marginal benefit is the enjoyment or satisfaction gained from eating one more cookie, while the marginal cost is the negative consequence, such as feeling too full or the cost of purchasing more cookies. The consumer will continue to eat cookies as long as the marginal benefit of eating one more cookie is greater than or equal to the marginal cost. Once the enjoyment of another cookie no longer outweighs the cost, the consumer will decide to stop eating. This process helps the consumer maximize their overall satisfaction while managing their resources effectively.
\\ \textit{Note:} correct because it accurately describes how a rational consumer evaluates the trade-off between the additional satisfaction gained from consuming one more cookie and the drawbacks associated with that consumption, ultimately deciding to stop when the marginal benefit no longer exceeds the marginal cost.

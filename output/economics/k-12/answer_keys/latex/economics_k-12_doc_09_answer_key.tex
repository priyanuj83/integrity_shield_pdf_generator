\section*{Long Form Response}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\setcounter{enumi}{10}
\item \textbf{Answer:} Rising price levels, or inflation, significantly impact individuals with fixed money incomes, such as retirees or those on a set salary. These individuals receive the same amount of money regardless of price increases in goods and services. As prices rise, their purchasing power decreases, meaning they can buy less with the same amount of money. This situation creates challenges, as their essential expenses, like food, housing, and healthcare, become harder to afford. Consequently, people with fixed incomes often struggle to maintain their standard of living during periods of inflation, making them particularly vulnerable in such economic conditions.
\\ \textit{Note:} correct because it highlights that individuals with fixed money incomes experience a decline in purchasing power during inflation, making it increasingly difficult for them to afford essential goods and services as prices rise.
\item \textbf{Answer:} Oligopoly is a market structure characterized by a small number of firms that dominate the market, leading to interdependent decision-making regarding pricing and output. In this scenario, firms often engage in strategic behavior, such as collusion or price wars, to maintain their market position. The condition P = MC, which states that price equals marginal cost, typically does not apply in an oligopoly because firms have significant market power and can set prices above marginal cost to maximize profits. This ability to influence prices arises from the limited competition and the strategic interactions between the few firms, resulting in higher prices and lower output compared to more competitive market structures.
\\ \textit{Note:} correct because it accurately identifies the defining features of oligopoly, including the influence of a few dominant firms on pricing and output decisions, and it explains that the market power these firms possess allows them to set prices above marginal cost, thus violating the condition P = MC.
\end{enumerate}

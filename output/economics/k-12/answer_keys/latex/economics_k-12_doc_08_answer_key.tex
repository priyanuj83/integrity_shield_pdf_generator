\item \textbf{Answer:} A trade surplus occurs when a country exports more goods and services than it imports, leading to a positive balance in the current account. This surplus indicates that the country is earning more from its foreign trade than it is spending on imports, which can strengthen its currency and improve its overall economic health. As a result, the financial account may reflect increased foreign investment in the country or a rise in savings, as the surplus allows for greater capital accumulation. However, if the surplus persists, it can lead to tensions with trade partners and potential retaliatory measures. Overall, a trade surplus positively impacts the current account while influencing the financial account through changes in investment patterns.

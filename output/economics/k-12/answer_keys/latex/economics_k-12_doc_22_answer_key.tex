\item \textbf{Answer:} In a perfectly competitive labor market, equilibrium occurs when the supply of labor matches the demand for labor, which means that the number of workers willing to work at a given wage is equal to the number of jobs available. This balance ensures that everyone who wants to work has the opportunity to do so, as employers hire all qualified candidates at the market wage. When wages are flexible and adjust to changes in demand and supply, it creates an environment where job seekers can find employment. Essentially, this equilibrium helps prevent unemployment caused by a mismatch between job availability and worker supply, allowing individuals to participate in the labor force and contribute to the economy.
\\ \textit{Note:} correct because it outlines how the equilibrium in a perfectly competitive labor market occurs when labor supply equals labor demand, ensuring that all willing workers can find jobs at the prevailing market wage.
\item \textbf{Answer:} Diminishing marginal utility is the economic principle that as a person consumes more of a good, the additional satisfaction or utility gained from each subsequent unit decreases. This concept explains consumer behavior when the price of a good increases; as prices rise, consumers may buy less of the good because the additional satisfaction they receive does not justify the higher cost. For example, if someone enjoys pizza, the first slice provides a lot of satisfaction, but the fifth slice may not be as enjoyable. Therefore, when the price of pizza goes up, consumers might choose to buy fewer slices or switch to a different food that provides better value for their money. This behavior reflects how diminishing marginal utility influences decisions in response to changing prices.
\\ \textit{Note:} The answer correctly identifies diminishing marginal utility as the principle that explains how increased prices lead consumers to reduce their quantity demanded, as the additional satisfaction from consuming more of a good does not outweigh its higher cost.

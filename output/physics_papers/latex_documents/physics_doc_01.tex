\section*{Multiple Choice (20 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item The pressure at the bottom of a pond does NOT depend on the
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item acceleration due to gravity.
    \item water density.
    \item depth of the pond.
    \item surface area of the pond.
\end{enumerate}
\item Heat engines, such as a jet engine, are more efficient when run at
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item high temperatures
    \item constant temperatures
    \item low temperatures
    \item a constant rate
\end{enumerate}
\item An ideal battery of constant voltage V supplies current to a circuit that has total resistance R. If R is doubled, what will happen to the total power...
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item It will double.
    \item It will quadruple.
    \item It will be halved.
    \item It will remain the same.
\end{enumerate}
\item The fact that the Moon always shows its same face to Earth is evidence that the Moon rotates about its axis about once per
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item day
    \item month
    \item year
    \item None of these for the moon does not rotate about an axis
\end{enumerate}
\item How many electrons are there in the third shell of sodium, Na (atomic number 11)?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item None
    \item One
    \item Two
    \item Three
\end{enumerate}
\item What happens to the pressure, P, of an ideal gas if the temperature is increased by a factor of 2 and the volume is increased by a factor of 8 ?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item P decreases by a factor of 16.
    \item P decreases by a factor of 4.
    \item P decreases by a factor of 2.
    \item P increases by a factor of 4.
\end{enumerate}
\item A pair of protective headphones reduces the sound of a jet engine from 140 dB to 110 dB. What percent of the noise is being blocked?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 99.90\%
    \item 78.60\%
    \item 21.40\%
    \item 0.10\%
\end{enumerate}
\item Heat comes from the Sun to Earth by the process of
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item conduction
    \item convection
    \item radiation
    \item All of these, although radiation is greatest
\end{enumerate}
\item The mutual induction of electric and magnetic fields can produce
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item light
    \item energy
    \item Both of these
    \item Neither of these
\end{enumerate}
\item Immediately after two separated charged particles are released from rest both increase in speed. The particles therefore have
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the same sign of charge.
    \item opposite signs of charge.
    \item either the same or opposite signs of charge.
    \item Need more information.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

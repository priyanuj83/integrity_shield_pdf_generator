Both religions teach the importance of self-discipline, non-violence, and the renunciation of worldly attachments. However, their beliefs diverge in key areas: Jainism emphasizes strict asceticism and the concept of an eternal soul (jiva), while Buddhism focuses on the impermanence of all things and the idea of no-self (anatta). Historically, Jainism has maintained a strong focus on rituals and community, whereas Buddhism evolved into various schools, each interpreting the teachings of Siddhartha Gautama, the Buddha, in different ways.

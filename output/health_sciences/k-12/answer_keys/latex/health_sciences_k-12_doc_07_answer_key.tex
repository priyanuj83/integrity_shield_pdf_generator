\item \textbf{Answer:} Type 1 and type 2 diabetes are two distinct forms of diabetes that differ mainly in their causes and management. Type 1 diabetes is an autoimmune condition where the body's immune system mistakenly attacks and destroys the insulin-producing cells in the pancreas. This leads to a lack of insulin, requiring individuals to rely on insulin therapy for management. In contrast, type 2 diabetes is primarily linked to lifestyle factors and insulin resistance, not autoimmune processes. Treatment for type 2 often includes lifestyle changes and oral medications, while type 1 requires lifelong insulin administration. Understanding these differences is crucial for effective treatment and management of each type.

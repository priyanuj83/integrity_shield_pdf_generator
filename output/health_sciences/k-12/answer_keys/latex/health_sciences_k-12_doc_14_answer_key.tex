It stores excess energy in the form of triglycerides, which can be broken down and used for fuel when the body needs it, especially during periods of fasting or intense physical activity. Beyond energy storage, adipose tissue also helps regulate hormones that influence appetite and metabolism, thus impacting overall body weight and health. The balance of adipose tissue is significant; too little can lead to energy deficits, while excessive amounts can contribute to obesity and related health issues, such as diabetes and heart disease.

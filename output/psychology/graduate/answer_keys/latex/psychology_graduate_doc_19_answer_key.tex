For instance, if a person values health but smokes, they might either quit smoking or downplay the health risks associated with it to resolve the conflict. The implications for behavior change are significant, as understanding cognitive dissonance can facilitate interventions aimed at promoting healthier behaviors by highlighting inconsistencies between individuals' actions and their self-identified values.

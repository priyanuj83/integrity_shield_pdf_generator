\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Pat is learning how energy flows through an ecosystem. Pat understands that animals get energy from plants. What do plants do that allow animals to get energy from them?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Plants keep water in their roots.
    \item Plants release nitrogen into the soil.
    \item Plants provide shelter from the weather.
    \item Plants make sugar in the presence of light.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of these is an example of an adaptation for defense from predators?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item giraffe's long neck
    \item baby deer's spots
    \item otter's oily fur
    \item fish's gills
\end{enumerate}
\item Which planet in the solar system has the longest year?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The planet closest to the Sun.
    \item The planet with the longest day.
    \item The planet with the most moons.
    \item The planet farthest from the Sun.
\end{enumerate}
\item A student poured water into a plastic tray. The student then put the tray into the freezer. What property of the water changed as the water froze?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The water became a gas.
    \item The mass of water increased.
    \item The water took a definite shape.
    \item The flavor of the water changed.
\end{enumerate}
\item Rossby waves are slow-moving, long ocean waves. What feature of a Rossby wave would change if the wavelength was doubled?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The amplitude would double.
    \item The frequency would double.
    \item The amplitude would decrease by half.
    \item The frequency would decrease by half.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item In a laboratory procedure, a student observes different sizes of soil particles. Which tool is best for the investigation?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item a hand lens with 1 X magnification
    \item a microscope with 40 X magnification
    \item a light microscope with 100 X magnification
    \item a dissecting microscope with 4 X magnification
\end{enumerate}
\item What process causes igneous rock to transform into metamorphic rock?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item deposition in water
    \item slow cooling and crystallization
    \item weathering and erosion
    \item exposure to heat and high pressure
\end{enumerate}
\item How many basic units of information in a DNA molecule are required to encode a single amino acid?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 1
    \item 2
    \item 3
    \item 4
\end{enumerate}
\item When a button is pushed on a battery-operated clock, the time display is illuminated. What process has taken place?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Pressure has been converted to light.
    \item Force has been converted to light.
    \item Closing an electric circuit has produced light.
    \item Opening an electric circuit has produced light.
\end{enumerate}
\item A mouse is placed at one end of a cardboard maze that has a piece of cheese at the other end. The mouse smells the cheese and learns how to find it. The mouse is using an
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item acquired trait to gain an inherited trait.
    \item inherited trait to gain an acquired trait.
    \item acquired trait to gain another acquired trait.
    \item inherited trait to gain another inherited trait.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

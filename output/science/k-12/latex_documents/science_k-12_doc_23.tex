\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}

\item In a forest, how do decomposers help other organisms survive?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item They release oxygen into the air that animals breathe.
    \item They put nutrients into the soil that plants use to grow.
    \item They provide shelter in forests where animals can hide.
    \item They use sunlight to make food for plants and animals.
\end{enumerate}
\item The Moon lacks weather and climate changes like those on Earth. What causes the lack of weather on the Moon?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

    \item the very thin atmosphere
    \item the lack of magnetic poles
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item a filter
    \item a measuring cup
    \item a balance
    \item a magnifying glass
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

    \item volcanic eruption
    \item movement of plates
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

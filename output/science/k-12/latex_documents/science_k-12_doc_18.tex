\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item A group of students observed the formation of bubbles when water boiled. What happened to matter because of the physical change to the water?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item New matter was created in the bubbles.
    \item Old matter was destroyed as the water turned to gas.
    \item Matter was conserved when it changed form.
    \item The composition of the matter changed.
\end{enumerate}
\item Why does sexual reproduction result in more genetic diversity than asexual reproduction?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Traits from two parents are combined.
    \item More organisms reproduce this way.
    \item Offspring grow in different environments.
    \item Offspring come from identical parents.
\end{enumerate}
\item Paramecium and volvox can both reproduce sexually. How is sexual reproduction in volvox different than sexual reproduction in paramecium?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Volvox colonies produce gametes.
    \item Volvox cells undergo conjugation.
    \item Volvox colonies produce single-celled offspring.
    \item Volvox cells reproduce by vegetative budding.
\end{enumerate}
\item The human population is increasing every day. How does this increase in population affect the natural resources that maintain humans?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item increases the supply of resources
    \item decreases the amount of resources
    \item results in less demand for resources
    \item improves the quality of the resources
\end{enumerate}
\item All of these are examples of the ways Earth and the Moon interact except
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the phases of the Moon.
    \item the tides on Earth.
    \item seasons on Earth.
    \item lunar eclipses.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Data in tables may also be presented in graphs. Which type of data would best be displayed on a circle graph?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the distance of the planets from the sun
    \item the depths of the major oceans on Earth
    \item the amount of rainfall each day for a month
    \item the percent of various materials in solid waste
\end{enumerate}
\item Forests have been cut and burned so that the land can be used to raise crops. Which consequence does this activity have on the atmosphere of Earth?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item It reduces the amount of carbon dioxide production.
    \item It reduces the production of oxygen.
    \item It decreases the greenhouse effect.
    \item It decreases pollutants in the air.
\end{enumerate}
\item A student wants to use a stream table to test the influence of water acidity on the erosion rate of a stream. Which variable should be constant in this experiment?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item angle of tilt of the table
    \item acidity of the water
    \item amount of sediment eroded
    \item rate that sediment is eroded
\end{enumerate}
\item Some animals live in a grassland. A fire burns through the grassland. Which type of animal will best compete for resources after the fire?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item one that eats meat
    \item one that uses plants for shelter
    \item one that lives under the ground
    \item one that builds nests on the ground
\end{enumerate}
\item A boy can see his face when he looks into a calm pond. Which physical property of the pond makes this happen?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item flexibility
    \item reflectiveness
    \item temperature
    \item volume
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

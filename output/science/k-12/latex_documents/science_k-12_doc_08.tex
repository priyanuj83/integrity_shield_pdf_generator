    \item 0
    \item 1
    \item 4
    \item 7
\end{enumerate}
\item Which land form is the result of the constructive force of a glacier?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item valleys carved by a moving glacier
    \item piles of rocks deposited by a melting glacier
    \item grooves created in a granite surface by a glacier
    \item bedrock hills roughened by the passing of a glacier
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 15^\ensuremath{\circC}
    \item 21^\ensuremath{\circC}
    \item 27^\ensuremath{\circC}
    \item 33^\ensuremath{\circC}
\end{enumerate}

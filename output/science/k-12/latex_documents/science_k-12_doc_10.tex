\item What do the energy resources uranium and coal have in common?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item They both are formed from the remains of dead organisms.
    \item They both are acquired by mining the lithosphere.
    \item They both release energy through combustion.

    \item an increase in precipitation
    \item a decrease in pollutants
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item There is less rainfall in the desert.
    \item Temperatures vary more in the desert.
    \item There is less water vapor in the desert air.
    \item There are fewer plants in the desert.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

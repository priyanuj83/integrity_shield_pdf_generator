\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Life on Earth originated from
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item We don't know for sure.
    \item aliens (panspermia).
    \item comets.
    \item the Earth's oceans.
\end{enumerate}
\item What is true about the rotation of a solid disk?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The velocity is the same on every point on the disk
    \item The part closer to the axis has a longer period
    \item The period of rotation is the same on every point on the disk
    \item The part closer to the axis has a shorter period
\end{enumerate}
\item A comet of mass m impacts the earth (mass M radius R) at the minimum impact speed. What is the expression for the total energy released in the impact?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item m*v
    \item 0.5*m/($R^3$)
    \item 0.5*m*(2 GM/R)
    \item 0.6*G($M^2$)/R
\end{enumerate}
\item The distance between the Earth and the star Altair is one million times greater than the distance between the Earth and the Sun. How far is Altair from the Earth?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 9.3 x 1013 meters
    \item 9.3 x 1010 meters
    \item 1.5 x 1014 meters
    \item 1.5 x 1017 meters
\end{enumerate}
\item What do meteorites reveal about the solar system?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item They reveal that the early solar system consisted mostly of hydrogen and helium gas.
    \item They reveal that meteorites are much older than the comets and planets.
    \item They reveal that the age of the solar system is approximately 4.6 billion years.
    \item They reveal that the solar system once contained 10 planets.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

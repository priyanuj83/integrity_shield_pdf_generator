\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following was not cited as evidence for life in the martian meteorite ALH 84001?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Complex organic molecules specifically PAHs
    \item Magnetite grains similar to those formed by bacteria on Earth
    \item Carbonate minerals indicating a thicker warmer Martian atmosphere
    \item Amino acids with a preferred orientation or "chirality"
\end{enumerate}
\item What Mars mission will be landing on May 25 2008 and will dig a trench into (hopefully) ice-rich soil?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item ExoMars
    \item Mars Exploration Rovers
    \item Mars Science Laboratory
    \item Phoenix Mars Lander
\end{enumerate}
\item You observe the visible spectrum of a star viewed through a cloud of cool hydrogen gas.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item You see only the star's blackbody spectrum.
    \item You see the star's blackbody spectrum with absorption lines due to hydrogen.
    \item You see only emission lines characteristic of hydrogen.
    \item You see only emission lines characteristic of the star's composition.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following countries has sent landers to Venus?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The U.S.S.R.
    \item The U.S.
    \item France
    \item A and B
\end{enumerate}
\item By locating the north celestial pole (NCP) in the sky how can you determine your latitude?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The azimuth of the NCP is the angular distance from the North Pole.
    \item The azimuth of the NCP is the same as your latitude.
    \item The altitude of the NCP is the same as your latitude.
    \item The altitude of the NCP is your angular distance from the North Pole.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

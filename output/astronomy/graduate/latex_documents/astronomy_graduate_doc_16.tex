\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Say the pupil of your eye has a diameter of 5 mm and you have a telescope with an aperture of 50 cm. How much more light can the telescope gather than your eye?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 10000 times more
    \item 100 times more
    \item 1000 times more
    \item 10 times more
\end{enumerate}
\item What is true about the rotation of a solid disk?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The velocity is the same on every point on the disk
    \item The part closer to the axis has a longer period
    \item The period of rotation is the same on every point on the disk
    \item The part closer to the axis has a shorter period
\end{enumerate}
\item The resolution of a telescope is a measure of its:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Ability to magnify distant objects
    \item Ability to measure the angular separation of objects
    \item Ability to measure the distance between objects
    \item Light-collecting efficiency
\end{enumerate}
\item A comet of mass m impacts the earth (mass M radius R) at the minimum impact speed. What is the expression for the total energy released in the impact?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item m*v
    \item 0.5*m/($R^3$)
    \item 0.5*m*(2 GM/R)
    \item 0.6*G($M^2$)/R
\end{enumerate}
\item Find the best approximation for the surface temperature of the Sun:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 6000 K
    \item 7000 K
    \item 9000 K
    \item 13000 K
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

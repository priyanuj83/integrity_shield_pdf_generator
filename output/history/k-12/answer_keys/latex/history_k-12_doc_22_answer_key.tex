\item \textbf{Answer:} Parietal and mobile art from the Upper Paleolithic period demonstrate significant advancements in human intellect and provide insights into the culture of that time. Mobile art, such as small carvings and figurines, reflects creativity and skill in manipulating materials. Together, these art forms indicate that Upper Paleolithic societies valued artistic expression and storytelling, which likely played a role in social cohesion and the transmission of knowledge. This artistic development reveals a culture rich in imagination and communication, highlighting the cognitive evolution of early humans.

The development of Uruk facilitated advancements in technology, trade, and culture, leading to innovations such as writing and monumental architecture.

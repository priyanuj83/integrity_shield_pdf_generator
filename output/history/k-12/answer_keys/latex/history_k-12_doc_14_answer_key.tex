\item \textbf{Answer:} Absolute dating and relative dating are two methods used to determine the age of historical events and artifacts. Absolute dating provides a specific year or range of years, such as determining that a certain artifact is from 2500 BCE. In contrast, relative dating places events or objects in a sequence without assigning a specific date, like stating that an artifact is older than another found nearby. Understanding these differences is significant because absolute dating gives a clearer picture of timelines, while relative dating helps to understand the context and relationship between various historical events. Together, they enhance our comprehension of history by allowing us to piece together a more complete timeline of human development.
\\ \textit{Note:} The answer correctly distinguishes between absolute dating, which provides specific dates, and relative dating, which establishes a chronological sequence, thereby illustrating their respective roles in understanding historical timelines.

\section*{Multiple Choice (20 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item In which of the following the idea of 'natural law' first appeared
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item French Revolution
    \item American War of Independence
    \item Roman Law
    \item Greek Thinking
\end{enumerate}
\item The role of gathering and interpreting intelligence about foreign countries in order to allow policymakers to make good foreign policy decisions was given to
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA).
    \item the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI).
    \item the National Security Council.
    \item Both A and B are correct.
\end{enumerate}
\item Under common law principles, which of the following is not correct regarding the duty owed by a possessor of land towards an undiscovered trespasser?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item No duty for artificial conditions.
    \item A duty for natural conditions.
    \item No duty for natural conditions.
    \item No duty for active operations.
\end{enumerate}
\item Client thinks she has been slandered. What of the following is not true about slander?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item It is spoken defamation.
    \item Plaintiff has to prove special damages, unless it falls into slander per se.
    \item The statement does not have to be published if it constitutes slander per se.
    \item There are four slander per se categories.
\end{enumerate}
\item "Natural law is based on the nature of man and on his inward need of living in society." Who said it?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Hugo Grotius
    \item Hobbes
    \item Locke
    \item Rousseau
\end{enumerate}
\item Bureaucratic politics suggests we should be worried about which of the following with regard to nuclear weapons?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Having the capability to deter the most powerful rival
    \item Having the capability to deter smaller states
    \item How nuclear attacks are identified and responded to; who controls the weapons
    \item Bureaucratic politics provides no information about nuclear proliferation and use
\end{enumerate}
\item Who was the first jurist to study comparative aspect of law?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Savigny
    \item Austin
    \item Kelsen
    \item Maine
\end{enumerate}
\item The foreign policy doctrine that European nations should stay out of Latin America is known as
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the Powell Doctrine.
    \item the Roosevelt Doctrine.
    \item the Bush Doctrine.
    \item the Monroe Doctrine.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is a legitimate threat to societal security?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Political policies designed to alter demographic characteristics of a state.
    \item All of these options.
    \item Legislation that criminalises certain cultural behaviours or practice.
    \item The use of military force to conduct ethnic-cleansing through displacement and killing.
\end{enumerate}
\item In which situation would a Defendant not be held to the standard of care of a reasonable person?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Defendant is deaf, and is accused of being negligent in failing to heed a warning bell.
    \item Defendant has poor judgment.
    \item Defendant is hot-tempered and damaged property in a tantrum.
    \item Defendant has an I.Q. of 85.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\item True or False: The correct answer to 'Which of the following constitutional provisions is applicable to corporations?' is 'The equal protection clause of the Fourteenth Amendment.'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'The enforcement mechanism of the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (and Protocol) consists of' is 'A reporting mechanism only'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'Who is considered the "father" of containment?' is 'George Kennan'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'Should treaties assume a particular form?' is 'Treaties have to be designated as such, no matter what form they assume'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'Which of the examples below has not been developed by the European Court of Human Rights as a situation where the state has a positive...' is 'When the applicant is in the custody of the state'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'Which of the following articles are not qualified rights?' is 'Article 9'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'Which of the following did not emerge as a trend of the defence trade in the 20th century?' is 'A decrease in the arms trade.'.

\item True or False: The correct answer to 'American realists argued that formalism devalued the authority of the judge to make law, because:' is 'The structure of legal reasoning is flawed.'.
\end{enumerate}

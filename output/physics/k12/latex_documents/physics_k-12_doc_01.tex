\section*{Multiple Choice (20 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item The lowest possible temperature is absolute zero, at
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 0 kelvin
    \item -273$^\circ$C
    \item Both are the same
    \item None of these
\end{enumerate}
\item A magnetic force can act on an electron even when it
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item is at rest
    \item moves parallel to magnetic field lines
    \item Both of these
    \item Neither of these

\item Which of these quantities decreases as the inverse square of distance for distances far from the objects producing the fields?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the electric field produced by a finite-length charged rod
    \item the electric field produced by an infinitely long charged cylinder
    \item the electric field produced by an infinite plane of charge
    \item the magnetic field produced by an infinitely long, straight current-carrying wire
\end{enumerate}
\item When a wave moves from shallow water to deep water, the
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item frequency decreases, the wavelength increases, and the speed increases
    \item frequency does not change, the wavelength increases, and the speed increases
    \item frequency does not change, the wavelength decreases, and the speed decreases
    \item frequency does not change, the wavelength increases, and the speed decreases
\end{enumerate}
\item In the absence of air resistance a ball of mass m is tossed upward to reach a height of 20 m. At the 10-m position half way up the net force on the ball is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 2mg
    \item mg
    \item mg/2
    \item mg/4
\end{enumerate}
\item As more lamps are connected to a series circuit, the current in the power source
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item increases
    \item decreases
    \item remains the same
    \item None of these
\end{enumerate}
\item An alarm whose frequency is 400 Hz is dropped out of a third-floor window. The student who drops it measures the frequency with a very sensitive...
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item appears higher than 400 Hz and the frequency increase as it falls
    \item appears higher than 400 Hz and the frequency decreases as it falls
    \item appears lower than 400 Hz and the frequency decreases as it falls
    \item appears lower than 400 Hz and the frequency increases as it falls

\item The power required to raise a 100-kg crate a vertical distance of 2 m in a time of 4 s is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 200 W
    \item 500 W
    \item 800 W
    \item 2000 W
\end{enumerate}
\item During the time an aircraft produces a sonic boom, the aircraft is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item breaking the sound barrier
    \item pulling out of a subsonic dive
    \item flying faster than sound
    \item All of these
\end{enumerate}
\item If you travel at high speed, then compared with your friends who "stay at home," you are

\section*{True / False (20 marks)}
\textit{Answer True or False and justify briefly.}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'Compared with a fundamental tone, the frequency of its second harmonic is' is 'half as much'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'A model airplane flies slower when flying into the wind and faster with wind at its back. When launched at right angles to the wind a...' is 'greater'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'A galvanometer can be calibrated to read electric' is 'voltage'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'According to four-dimensional geometry, the angles of a triangle add to 180$^\circ$' is 'sometimes.'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'The "greenhouse gases" that contribute to global warming absorb' is 'more infrared radiation than visible.'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'An object of mass 2 kg is acted upon by three external forces, each of magnitude 4 N. Which of the following could NOT be the resulting...' is '4 m/s\^{}2'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'A hologram best illustrates' is 'superposition'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'A big fish swims upon and swallows a small fish that is at rest. Right after lunch, the fattened big fish has a change in' is 'Both'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'The Doppler effect occurs when a source of sound moves' is 'Both of these'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'According to Hooke's law, if you hang by a tree branch and note how much it bends, then hanging with twice the weight produces' is 'the same bend if the branch doesn't break.'.
\end{enumerate}

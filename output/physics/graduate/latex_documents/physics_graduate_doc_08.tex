\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item A sound wave with frequency f travels through air at speed v. With what speed will a sound wave with frequency 4 f travel through the air?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item $v^2$
    \item v/4
    \item v/2
    \item 4 v
    \item 4 $v^2$
\end{enumerate}
\item A$_{60}$-ohm electric lamp is left connected to a 240-volt line for 3.00 min. How much energy is taken from the line?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 1.2 \ensuremath{\times} $10^4$ Joules
    \item 3.6 \ensuremath{\times} $10^4$ Joules
    \item 2.2 \ensuremath{\times} $10^4$ Joules
    \item 1.72 \ensuremath{\times} $10^4$ Joules
    \item 1.9 \ensuremath{\times} $10^4$ Joules
\end{enumerate}
\item As more lamps are connected in parallel in a circuit, the current in the power source
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item remains the same
    \item Not enough information to say
    \item is not affected
    \item triples
    \item decreases
\end{enumerate}
\item Two spheres of net charge +5 e and -6 e briefly come into contact. Afterward, which of the following is a possible combination of net charges for the two spheres?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Both of the above
    \item +3 e and -4 e
    \item +5 e and -6 e
    \item +2.5 e and -3.5 e
    \item +4 e and -5 e
\end{enumerate}
\item During the time an aircraft produces a sonic boom the aircraft is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Breaking the speed of light
    \item Flying slower than sound
    \item pulling out of a subsonic dive
    \item flying faster than sound
    \item Flying at constant speed
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item What is the force between two positive charges of 100\ensuremath{\mu} coulombs and 200\ensuremath{\mu} coulombs, respectively, when they are separated a distance of 3 centimeters?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 5 x $10^5$ nt
    \item 1 x $10^5$ nt
    \item 7 x $10^5$ nt
    \item 3 x $10^5$ nt
    \item 6 x $10^5$ nt
\end{enumerate}
\item The direction of natural processes is from states of
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item lower order to higher order
    \item chaos to equilibrium
    \item higher order to lower order
    \item disorder to organization
    \item equilibrium to higher order
\end{enumerate}
\item Any atom that emits an alpha particle or beta particle
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item changes its atomic number but remains the same element.
    \item becomes an atom of a different element always.
    \item remains the same element and isotope.
    \item becomes a different element with the same atomic mass.
    \item becomes an atom of a different element sometimes.
\end{enumerate}
\item The separation of the headlights on a car is about 150 cm. When the car is 30 meters away, what is the separation of the images of the lights on the retina?

    \item 4 mm
    \item .833 mm
    \item 1 mm
    \item 1.5 mm
    \item 0.5 mm
\end{enumerate}
\item A hoist raises a load of 330 pounds a distance of 15 feet in 5 seconds. At what rate in horsepower is the hoist working?

    \item 2.7 hp
    \item 1.8 hp
    \item 1.2 hp
    \item 3.0 hp
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

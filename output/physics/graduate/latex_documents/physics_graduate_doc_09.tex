\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item What is the total binding energy and the binding energy per nucleon of the nucleus of \_24 $Cr^52$ which has a mass of 51.95699 amu?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 4.0 \ensuremath{\times} $10^8$ eV and 7.7 \ensuremath{\times} $10^6$ eV
    \item 4.2 \ensuremath{\times} $10^8$ eV and 8.1 \ensuremath{\times} $10^6$ eV
    \item 4.6 \ensuremath{\times} $10^8$ eV and 8.9 \ensuremath{\times} $10^7$ eV
    \item 4.6 \ensuremath{\times} $10^8$ eV and 8.9 \ensuremath{\times} $10^6$ eV
    \item 5.0 \ensuremath{\times} $10^8$ eV and 9.6 \ensuremath{\times} $10^6$ eV
\end{enumerate}
\item A car covers a distance of 30 miles in 1/2 hour. What is its speed in miles per hour and in feet per second?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 30 mi per hr, 60 ft per sec
    \item 90 mi per hr, 132 ft per sec
    \item 75 mi per hr, 110 ft per sec
    \item 60 mi per hr, 55 ft per sec
    \item 45 mi per hr, 88 ft per sec
\end{enumerate}
\item An object of mass 100 g is at rest. A net force of 2000 dynes is applied for 10 sec. What is the final velocity? How far will the object have moved in the 10-sec interval?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 150 cm/sec and 7.5 m
    \item 350 cm/sec and 17.5 m
    \item 450 cm/sec and 22.5 m
    \item 250 cm/sec and 12.5 m
    \item 200 cm/sec and 10 m
\end{enumerate}
\item What is the ratio of intensities of Rayleigh scattering for the two hydrogen lines \ensuremath{\lambda}\_1 = 410 nm and \ensuremath{\lambda}\_2 = 656 nm?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 4.23:1
    \item 10.24:1
    \item 8.67:1
    \item 2.98:1
    \item 5.67:1
\end{enumerate}
\item Why can't we see the Sun's corona except during total solar eclipses?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item We can't see magnetic fields
    \item The corona is blocked by the Earth's atmosphere
    \item The corona is hidden by the Sun's photosphere
    \item The corona is made up mostly of charged particles which aren't luminous.
    \item The Sun's corona is actually invisible to the human eye
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

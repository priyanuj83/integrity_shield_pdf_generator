\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Light of wavelength 400 nm is incident upon lithium (phi = 2.93 eV). Calculate the stopping potential in V.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 0.87
    \item -0.23
    \item 0.07
    \item 0.57
    \item 0.97
\end{enumerate}
\item A voltage will be induced in a wire loop when the magnetic field within that loop
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item is amplified
    \item converts to magnetic energy
    \item is parallel to the electric field
    \item changes
    \item is at right angles to the electric field
\end{enumerate}
\item Calculate the binding energy of the hydrogen atom in its ground state.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 14.1 eV
    \item 10.6 eV
    \item 11.2 eV
    \item 17.0 eV
    \item 15.3 eV
\end{enumerate}
\item $B^10$ is bombarded with neutrons, and \ensuremath{\alpha} particles are observed to be emitted. What is the residual nucleus?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item ^8\_4 Be
    \item ^13\_6 C
    \item ^7\_4 Be
    \item ^6\_3 Li
    \item ^5\_2 He
\end{enumerate}
\item The quantum efficiency of a photon detector is 0.1. If$_{100}$ photons are sent into the detector, one after the other, the detector will detect photons
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item an average of 10 times, with an rms deviation of about 5
    \item an average of 10 times, with an rms deviation of about 3
    \item an average of 10 times, with an rms deviation of about 0.1
    \item an average of 10 times, with an rms deviation of about 1
    \item an average of 10 times, with an rms deviation of about 6
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

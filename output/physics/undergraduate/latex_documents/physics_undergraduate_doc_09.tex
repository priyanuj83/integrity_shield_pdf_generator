\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item The quantum efficiency of a photon detector is 0.1. If$_{100}$ photons are sent into the detector, one after the other, the detector will detect photons
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item exactly 10 times
    \item an average of 10 times, with an rms deviation of about 0.1
    \item an average of 10 times, with an rms deviation of about 1
    \item an average of 10 times, with an rms deviation of about 3
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following ions CANNOT be used as a dopant in germanium to make an n-type semiconductor?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item As
    \item P
    \item Sb
    \item B
\end{enumerate}
\item A satellite of mass m orbits a planet of mass M in a circular orbit of radius R. The time required for one revolution is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item independent of M
    \item proportional to $m^(1$/2)
    \item linear in R
    \item proportional to $R^(3$/2)
\end{enumerate}
\item A three-dimensional harmonic oscillator is in thermal equilibrium with a temperature reservoir at temperature T. The average total energy of the oscillator is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item (1/2) k T
    \item kT
    \item (3/2) k T
    \item 3 kT
\end{enumerate}
\item By definition, the electric displacement current through a surface S is proportional to the
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item rate of change of the electric flux through S
    \item electric flux through S
    \item time integral of the magnetic flux through S
    \item rate of change of the magnetic flux through S
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

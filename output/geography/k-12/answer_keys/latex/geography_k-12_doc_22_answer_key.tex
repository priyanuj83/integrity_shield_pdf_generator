This concept is particularly relevant for farm workers from other countries who travel to places like the United States during harvest seasons. Factors influencing circular migration include demand for labor in agriculture, seasonal work patterns, and economic conditions in both the home and host countries. By analyzing these maps, meteorologists can identify areas of high and low pressure, which play a crucial role in determining weather patterns. For example, low- pressure areas are often associated with stormy weather, while high-pressure systems typically bring clear skies. Understanding how atmospheric pressure affects wind movement and temperature helps forecasters predict weather changes more accurately.

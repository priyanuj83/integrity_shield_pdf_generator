\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item The inverse of -i in the multiplicative group, \{1, -1, i , -i\} is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 1
    \item -1
    \item i
    \item -i
\end{enumerate}
\item Find the order of the factor group (Z\_11 x Z\_15)/(\textless{}1, 1\textgreater{})
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 1
    \item 2
    \item 5
    \item 11
\end{enumerate}
\item The maximum number of acute angles in a convex 10-gon in the Euclidean plane is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 1
    \item 2
    \item 3
    \item 4
\end{enumerate}
\item Find the number of elements in the indicated cyclic group: The cyclic subgroup of Z\_30 generated by 25.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 25
    \item 5
    \item 6
    \item 30
\end{enumerate}
\item A total of x feet of fencing is to form three sides of a level rectangular yard. What is the maximum possible area of the yard, in terms of x ?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item $x^2$/9
    \item $x^2$/8
    \item $x^2$/4
    \item $x^2$
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

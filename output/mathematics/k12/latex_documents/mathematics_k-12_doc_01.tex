\section*{Multiple Choice (20 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Find 1 over 6 + 1 over 8.

    \item 7 over 24
    \item 2 over 14
    \item 1 over 7
    \item 1 over 4

\item The mean of 7 numbers is 15. When an 8th number is added, the mean decreases to 12. What is the 8th number?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item -12
    \item -9
    \item 0
    \item 8
\end{enumerate}
\item Suppose you toss a fair die three times and it comes up an even number each time. Which of the following is a true statement?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item By the law of large numbers, the next toss is more likely to be an odd number than another even number.
    \item Based on the properties of conditional probability the next toss is more likely to be an even number given that three in a row have been even.
    \item Dice actually do have memories, and thus the number that comes up on the next toss will be influenced by the previous tosses.
    \item The probability that the next toss will again be even is 0.5.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which is the best buy?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 3 bottles of juice for \$6.72
    \item 11 bottles of juice for \$24.86
    \item 9 bottles of juice for \$20.25
    \item 16 bottles of juice for \$35.68
\end{enumerate}
\item Simplify \$\textbackslash\{\}frac\{2+2i\}\{-3+4i\}\$. Express your answer as a complex number in the form \$a+bi\$, where \$a\$ and \$b\$ are real numbers.

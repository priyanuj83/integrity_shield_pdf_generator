\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item A meteorologist reports that there is a 30\% probability of rain and no sun. If there is a 40\% probability of no rain, then the probability of both rain and sun is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 0.16
    \item 0.24
    \item 0.3
    \item 0.5
\end{enumerate}
\item When $\sqrt[3]{-128}$ is simplified, the result is $a\sqrt[3]{b}$, where $a$ is an integer, and $b$ is a positive integer. If $b$ is as small as possible, then what is $a+b$?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 2
    \item -4
    \item 6
    \item -2
\end{enumerate}
\item Simplify $5^3$. Use a calculator, paper and pencil, or mental math.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 15
    \item 125
    \item 8
    \item 53
\end{enumerate}
\item Find the quotient of 5.04/0.7
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 0.72
    \item 0.702
    \item 10.0571
    \item 7.2
\end{enumerate}
\item Marguerite earned a score between 75 and 89 on all of her previous spelling tests. She earned a score of 100 on her next test. Which of the following statements is true?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The mode will increase.
    \item The mean will increase.
    \item The mean will decrease.
    \item The median will decrease.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

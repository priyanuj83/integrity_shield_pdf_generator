\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item The first number in a number pattern is 28. The pattern rule is to add 14 to get the next number in the pattern. If the pattern continues, which statement is true?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item All the numbers in the pattern can be divided equally by 10.
    \item All the numbers in the pattern can be divided equally by 4.
    \item All the numbers in the pattern can be divided equally by 8.
    \item All the numbers in the pattern can be divided equally by 7.
\end{enumerate}
\item Solve the equation 14 = w + 23.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item -37
    \item -9
    \item 9
    \item 37
\end{enumerate}
\item Erin made 12 pints of juice. She drinks 3 cups of juice each day. How many days will Erin take to drink all of the juice she made?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 2 days
    \item 4 days
    \item 8 days
    \item 9 days
\end{enumerate}
\item Mr. Jones rolls a six-sided cube numbered 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. What is the probability he rolls a three?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 6-Jan
    \item 5-Jan
    \item 3-Jan
    \item 2-Jan
\end{enumerate}
\item If $f(x) = 2$ for all real numbers $x$, what is the value of $f(x + 2)$?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 2
    \item 4
    \item 0
    \item 1
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

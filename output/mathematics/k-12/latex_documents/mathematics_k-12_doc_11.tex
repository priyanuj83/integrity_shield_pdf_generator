\end{enumerate}
\item Jonathan obtained a score of 80 on a statistics exam, placing him at the 90 th percentile. Suppose five points are added to everyone's score. Jonathan's new score will be at the
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 80 th percentile.
    \item 85 th percentile.
    \item 90 th percentile.
    \item 95 th percentile.
\end{enumerate}
\item Find the volume of the solid generated when the region bounded by the y-axis, y = $e^x$, and y = 2 is rotated around the y-axis.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 0.296
    \item 0.592
    \item 2.427
    \item 3.998
\end{enumerate}
\item How many positive cubes divide $3!\cdot 5!\cdot 7!\,$?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 6
    \item 4
    \item 3
    \item 1
\end{enumerate}
\item Two whole numbers have a least common multiple of 60. Each number is less than or equal to 12. The greatest common factor of the two numbersis 2. What are the two numbers?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 6 and 10
    \item 5 and 12
    \item 10 and 12
    \item 12 and 15
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

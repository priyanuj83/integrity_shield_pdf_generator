\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item If f(z) is an analytic function that maps the entire finite complex plane into the real axis, then the imaginary axis must be mapped onto
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item a circle
    \item an infinite interval
    \item the negative real axis
    \item the positive real axis
    \item a ray
\end{enumerate}
\item The original price of a new bicycle is \$138.00. If the bicycle is marked down 15\%, what is the new price?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item \$125.00
    \item \$20.70
    \item \$130.00
    \item \$133.00
    \item \$115.00
\end{enumerate}
\item Consider the initial value problem $ y^{\prime}+\frac{1}{4} y=3+2 \cos 2 t, \quad y(0)=0 $$ Determine the value of $t$ for which the solution first intersects the line $y=12\$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 11.234567
    \item 10.065778
    \item 6.789012
    \item 7.654321
    \item 9.876543
\end{enumerate}
\item Write the ratio of 96 runners to 216 swimmers in simplest form.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 16 over 36
    \item 9 over 4
    \item 8 over 18
    \item 48 over 108
    \item 24 over 54
\end{enumerate}
\item In the group G = \{2, 4, 6, 8) under multiplication modulo 10, the identity element is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 9
    \item 7
    \item 8
    \item 1
    \item 4
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\item True or False: The cumulative distribution function of the standard Gaussian distribution is not log-concave and is instead linear.
\item True or False: The double integral of the function f(x,y)=x*$y^3$ exp^\{$x^2$+$y^2$\} over I=[0,1]\ensuremath{\times}[0,1] evaluates to approximately 0.4295.

\item True or False: If a number is 12, then eighty-five more than its square equals the square of the quantity that is 17 less than the number.

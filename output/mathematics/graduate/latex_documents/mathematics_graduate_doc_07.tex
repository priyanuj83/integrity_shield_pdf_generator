\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which measurement would best be rounded to the nearest thousand?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The number of yards run by a player in a game
    \item The number of people at a professional football game
    \item The length of a football in inches
    \item The weight of a professional football player in pounds
    \item The number of touchdowns in a professional football season
\end{enumerate}
\item Maddie will ride her bike a total of 56 miles over 7 days. She will ride the same number of miles each day. What is the total number of miles Maddie will ride each day?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 9
    \item 63
    \item 56
    \item 8
    \item 28
\end{enumerate}
\item Let y = f(x) be a solution of the differential equation x dy + (y - $xe^x$) dx = 0 such that y = 0 when x = 1. What is the value of f(2)?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 2/e
    \item $e^2$ + 2
    \item 1/(2 e)
    \item $e^2$/2
    \item 2/($e^2$)
\end{enumerate}
\item Consider the two sets X = \{10, 30, 45, 50, 55, 70, 90\} and Y = \{10, 30, 35, 50, 65, 70, 90\}. Which of the following is false?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The sets have identical medians.
    \item The sets have identical means.
    \item The sets have identical ranges.
    \item None of the above are false.
\end{enumerate}
\item Tara can develop 2 rolls of film in about 18 minutes. At this rate, how long will it take her to develop 8 rolls of film?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 1 hour 44 minutes
    \item 1 hour 20 minutes
    \item 1 hour
    \item 1 hour 12 minutes
    \item 1 hour 30 minutes
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

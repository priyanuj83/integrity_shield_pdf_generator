\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Judge took judicial notice of some facts at the beginning of the trial. Which of the following is not an appropriate kind of fact for judicial notice?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Indisputable facts.
    \item Facts that have been asserted by individual political organizations.
    \item Facts recognized to be true by common knowledge.
    \item Facts capable of scientific verification.
\end{enumerate}
\item Seller has a contract that specifies a delivery point. What kind of a contract is this?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item F.A.S. Contract
    \item Destination contract
    \item Shipment contract
    \item F.O.B. Contract
\end{enumerate}
\item In order to have an irrevocable offer under the Uniform Commercial Code, the offer must
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Be made by a merchant to a merchant.
    \item Be contained in a signed writing which gives assurance that the offer will be held open.
    \item State the period of time for which it is irrevocable.
    \item Not be contained in a form supplied by the offeror.
\end{enumerate}
\item If a regulation does amount to a taking, then what must the government do?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Pay just compensation for the property
    \item Terminate the regulation and pay damages that occurred while regulation was in effect
    \item Pay double damages if not addressed in 30 days.
    \item a or b
\end{enumerate}
\item Which is the hierarchy of U.S. Law?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Executive agreements, U.S. Constitution, treaties and federal statutes, state law
    \item U.S constitution, treaties and federal statutes, executive agreements, state law
    \item Treaties and federal statutes, US Constitution, executive agreements, state law
    \item U.S constitution, executive agreements, treaties and federal statutes, state law
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

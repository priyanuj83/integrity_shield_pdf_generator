\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Hart argues that secondary rules are 'power-conferring' but this seems unconvincing in the case of the rule of recognition because:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

    \item It both confers power and imposes duties on judges to decide cases.

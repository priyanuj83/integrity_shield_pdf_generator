\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item The president, vice-president, and U.S. civil officers can be impeached. Which of the following is not true about impeachment?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Grounds include high crimes and misdemeanors
    \item A majority vote in the House is necessary to invoke charges of impeachment
    \item Grounds include treason and bribery
    \item A three-fourths vote in the Senate is necessary to convict and remove from office
\end{enumerate}
\item Hume's attack on natural law is founded on his argument that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item We cannot objectively know what is morally right or wrong.
    \item Natural law is backward-looking.
    \item There is no such thing as a social contract.
    \item Natural law fails to protect the state against attacks.
\end{enumerate}
\item There is a voting law that is going to be reviewed by the court. If the law is upheld, what must be true?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The law is necessary for a legitimate government purpose.
    \item The law is rationally related to an important government purpose.
    \item The law is unnecessarily related to a compelling government purpose.
    \item The law is substantially related to an important government purpose.
    \item The law is necessary to achieve a compelling government purpose.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following articles are not qualified rights?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Article 19
    \item Article 11
    \item Article 12
    \item Article 9
    \item Article 3
\end{enumerate}
\item Which statement is closest to characterizing the principal difference between the positions adopted by Hobbes and Locke?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item They differ in their views about the necessity of a social contract.
    \item They disagree about the role of law in society.
    \item They have contrasting views on the role of religion in society.
    \item They have different ideas about the importance of education.
    \item They have different perspectives on economic policies.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

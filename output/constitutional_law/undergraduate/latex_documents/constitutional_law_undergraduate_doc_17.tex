\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Are there any limits to the application of article 103 of the UN Charter?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item No, there are no limits to article 103
    \item Yes, Article 103 cannot trump multilateral treaties
    \item Yes, article 103 of the UN Charter cannot trump jus cogens norms
    \item Yes, when a case is submitted to the ICJ, article 103 ceases to have any effect
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following purposes does the 'internal point of view' play in Hart's concept of law?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item It distinguishes social rules from mere group habits.
    \item It defines the judicial function.
    \item It illustrates the authority of the legislature.
    \item It stresses the relationship between law and justice.
\end{enumerate}
\item What is the relationship between the formal sources of international law?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item There is no hierarchy between the formal sources of international law
    \item Treaties supersede custom
    \item Custom supersedes treaties
    \item General Principles of Law supersede both custom and treaties
\end{enumerate}
\item What does it mean for a State to be sovereign?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Sovereignty means being recognised by all other States
    \item Sovereignty means to be able to enter into treaties and join the UN
    \item Sovereignty means freedom to determine one's own affairs without external interference
    \item Sovereignty means possessing the right to defend oneself
\end{enumerate}
\item What is the purpose of universal jurisdiction?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Its aim is to establish certain offences as universal
    \item Its aim is to deter the commission of certain international crimes
    \item Its aim is to provide jurisdiction to all States in respect of particular crimes
    \item Its aim is to provide jurisdiction to all States in respect of all international crimes
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following best describes the practice of placing the blame for the problems of a society on one particular group?

    \item Stereotyping
    \item Scapegoating
    \item Institutionalization
\end{enumerate}
\item The population pyramid of a population that has had a slightly larger birth rate than death rate for several generations will most likely have which of the following shapes?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Wider at the top than in the middle
    \item Wider at the bottom than at the top
    \item Bulging in the middle
    \item Having parallel sides
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is the most important agent of socialization in early childhood?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A preschool provider
    \item A peer group
    \item The mass media
    \item The family
\end{enumerate}
\item The work of Smart suggested that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the rates of violent crime were similar for men and women
    \item women's sexual delinquency was more likely to be normalized than men's
    \item women's criminal behaviour tended to reflect traditional gender roles
    \item all of the above
\end{enumerate}
\item It is difficult to ascertain the true extent of domestic violence because:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

    \item there is no valid or reliable way of researching such a sensitive topic
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

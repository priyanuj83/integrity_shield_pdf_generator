\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Walt Disney, Sony and Time Warner are examples of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item transnational corporations
    \item multi-media empires
    \item ownership concentrated within one medium
    \item government-owned companies
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is not a regular national survey carried out by the British government?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Labour Force Survey
    \item General Household Survey
    \item Fashion Sensibility Survey
    \item Family Expenditure Survey
\end{enumerate}
\item Seven people standing silently while waiting for a bus at a bus stop is an example of which of the following?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A primary group
    \item A secondary group
    \item An aggregate
    \item A social category
\end{enumerate}
\item In the 1990 reform of the National Health Service, hospitals became:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item self-governing trusts competing for purchasing contracts from health authorities
    \item state-controlled providers, dependent on funding from the central government
    \item increasingly detached from health authorities and providers of private health care
    \item less inclined to run themselves efficiently, as demand for health care was falling
\end{enumerate}
\item In Marx's theory, the 'mode of production' means:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the way in which products are made in a factory
    \item the average measure of productivity under capitalism
    \item the organization of a society's technical and human resources
    \item an integral part of the superstructure of a society
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

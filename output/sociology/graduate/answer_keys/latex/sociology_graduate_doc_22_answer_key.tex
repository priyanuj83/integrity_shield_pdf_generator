In these environments, employees often perform repetitive tasks that require minimal skill and creativity, resulting in a sense of disconnection from the final product and a diminished sense of personal agency. This alienation not only affects employee morale and job satisfaction but can also lead to decreased productivity, as workers may feel less motivated to invest in their work when they see it as monotonous and devoid of meaning. The implications of this phenomenon are profound, suggesting that enhancing job enrichment and fostering a more engaged workforce could mitigate feelings of alienation and improve overall productivity in the manufacturing sector.

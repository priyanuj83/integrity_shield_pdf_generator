\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following did Thatcher's government not strive to achieve:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item marketization and privatization
    \item individual freedom, choice and responsibility
    \item generous financial benefits for single parents, students and the unemployed
    \item the rolling back of the welfare state
\end{enumerate}
\item 'Eyeballing' is a technique that involves:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item reading a textual document quickly to understand the gist of it
    \item gathering a sample from whoever you can see in a public place
    \item scanning a table to identify general patterns and significant figures
    \item keeping your eyes on research participants at all times
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following policies did the New Labour government not pursue?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item setting and streaming pupils by ability
    \item parental choice of school
    \item supporting LEAs that appeared to be failing
    \item state regulation and control of the curriculum
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is not a consequence of global tourism?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item decreased rates of prostitution and sex tourism
    \item developing countries can depend on it as a crucial source of income
    \item the exploitation of cheap, unregulated labour in poor countries
    \item we have become more aware of 'other' societies and ways of living
\end{enumerate}
\item How is terrorism different from the types of crime described by the Chicago School?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item it is committed on a larger, often global, scale, and is well organized
    \item it is associated with political conflict between states and their citizens
    \item it can have far-reaching effects upon international relations
    \item all of the above
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

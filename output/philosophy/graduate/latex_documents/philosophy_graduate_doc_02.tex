\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Identify the antecedent of the following conditional proposition: The Bees win their first game only if either the Aardvarks or the Chipmunks do not win their first games.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The Bees do not win their first game.
    \item Both the Aardvarks and the Chipmunks do not win their first games.
    \item The Chipmunks do not win their first game.
    \item The Chipmunks win their first game.
    \item Both the Aardvarks and the Chipmunks win their first games.
\end{enumerate}
\item Guru Nanak and his successors instructed the use of particular Sikh Ragas in order to promote which of the following?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Discipline and order
    \item Strength and perseverance
    \item Wisdom and knowledge
    \item Goodwill and love
    \item Courage and bravery
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is an example of a duty that corresponds to a right to goods and service?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item John's duty to return to Mary that car that he borrowed from her
    \item John's duty to allow Mary to pursue goals that she values
    \item John's duty to not harm Mary
    \item John's duty to not commit suicide
\end{enumerate}
\item Where is the key to liberation found within Sikhism?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item In faith in God
    \item In the strict adherence to the Five Ks
    \item In ascetic renunciation
    \item In the practice of yoga and other physical disciplines
    \item In acts of charity and service
\end{enumerate}
\item Anscombe criticizes as absurd Kant's idea of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the postulate of immortality.
    \item the autonomous will.
    \item the categorical imperative.
    \item legislating for oneself.
    \item the unity of consciousness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

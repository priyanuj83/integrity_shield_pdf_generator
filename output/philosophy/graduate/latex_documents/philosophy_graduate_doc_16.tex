\item When someone changes the meaning of a word or phrase from one part of the argument to another that person may have committed which of the following fallacies?

    \item Appeal to authority
    \item Red herring fallacy
    \item False cause fallacy
    \item Straw man fallacy

\item Singer claims that his argument upsets the traditional distinction between:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item fairness and bias.
    \item ethical and unethical.
    \item duty and charity.
    \item positive and negative rights.
    \item freedom and restriction.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

    \item our highest inward principle.

    \item the commands of God.
    \item the principles of our elders.
\end{enumerate}
\item In Singer's view, the question of when it's wrong to painlessly kill an animal is one

\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Baxter argues that we should view our treatment of the environment as a matter of various trade-offs whose aim is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item to minimize damage incurred by all individual living beings.
    \item to promote the functional integrity of ecosystems.
    \item to minimize the suffering of all sentient creatures.
    \item to ensure the survival of endangered species.
    \item to minimize damage to the ozone layer.
\end{enumerate}
\item Ross claims that the essential defect of utilitarianism is that it ignores
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the significance of moral intentions.
    \item epistemological responsibility.
    \item the role of virtue in morality.
    \item the value of pleasure.
    \item the importance of individual rights.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following fallacies happens when someone attributes the cause of an event to a simple cause when the cause is actually more complicated?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item slippery slope
    \item complex cause
    \item red herring
    \item ad hominem
    \item hasty generalization
\end{enumerate}
\item Cicero argues that we will never go morally astray if we:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item follow religious edicts.
    \item follow historical precedents.
    \item follow the teachings of philosophers.
    \item follow Nature as our guide.
    \item follow our conscience.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is not an example Aquinas gives of a self-evident proposition?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item "Man is a rational being."
    \item "All bachelors are unmarried."
    \item "Night follows day."
    \item "The sum of the angles of a triangle is 180 degrees."
    \item "The same thing cannot be affirmed and denied at the same time."
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

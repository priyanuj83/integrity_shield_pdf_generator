\item \textbf{Answer:} Corporate transparency refers to the openness and clarity with which a company communicates its operations, decisions, and financial performance to its stakeholders, including employees, customers, investors, and the community. This concept is significant in modern business practices as it fosters trust among stakeholders, which is crucial for long-term success. When organizations are transparent, they provide stakeholders with the information needed to make informed decisions, thereby enhancing accountability and reducing the potential for misunderstandings or mistrust. Furthermore, transparency can lead to stronger relationships, improved reputation, and ultimately, increased loyalty and support from stakeholders, contributing to sustainable business growth. In an era where consumers and investors demand ethical practices, corporate transparency has become a foundational element of effective business strategy.

\item \textbf{Answer:} Work specialization, also known as division of labor, refers to the practice of breaking down tasks into smaller, specialized segments assigned to different employees within an organization. This concept enhances productivity as it allows workers to focus on specific tasks where they can develop expertise, leading to increased efficiency and faster output. However, while work specialization can boost productivity, it can also lead to employee workload challenges, such as monotony and reduced job satisfaction, as individuals may perform repetitive tasks without variety. Balancing specialization with job enrichment strategies is crucial for maintaining employee motivation and optimizing overall organizational performance.
\\ \textit{Note:} The answer correctly identifies work specialization as a method of dividing tasks to improve efficiency and productivity, while also acknowledging the potential negative impacts on employee workload, such as monotony and reduced job satisfaction.

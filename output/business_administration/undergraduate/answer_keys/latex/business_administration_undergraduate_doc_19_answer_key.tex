This evolution necessitated a reinvention of traditional PR strategies, emphasizing transparency, responsiveness, and audience engagement. Furthermore, the accessibility of new media enabled stakeholders to become active participants in the dialogue, thereby demanding more authentic and interactive communication from brands. Consequently, PR 2.0 encapsulates the integration of these new media elements that redefine the role and practice of public relations in the digital age.

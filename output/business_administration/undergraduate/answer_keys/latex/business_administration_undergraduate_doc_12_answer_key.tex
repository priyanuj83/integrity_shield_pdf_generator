\item \textbf{Answer:} The typical order of managerial functions consists of planning, organizing, leading, and controlling, each playing a crucial role in effective management. Planning involves setting objectives and determining a course of action, providing a roadmap for the organization. Organizing entails arranging resources and tasks to implement the plan, ensuring efficiency and clarity in roles. Leading focuses on motivating and guiding employees, fostering a positive work environment and effective communication. Finally, controlling involves monitoring progress and making necessary adjustments to stay aligned with goals, ensuring that performance meets expectations. Together, these functions create a cohesive framework that drives organizational success.
\\ \textit{Note:} correct because it accurately outlines the sequential order of managerial functions-planning, organizing, leading, and controlling-and explains how each function contributes to achieving organizational goals and ensuring effective management practices.

\item \textbf{Answer:} The framework categorizing services in business administration includes three key properties: search, belief, and confidence properties. Search properties refer to characteristics of a service that can be evaluated before purchase, such as price and features, allowing consumers to make informed decisions. Belief properties encompass attributes that are assessed based on consumer perceptions and experiences, which can vary widely among individuals, thereby influencing brand loyalty and preferences. Lastly, confidence properties involve the trust and assurance a customer feels toward a service provider, often built through reputation and positive past interactions. Understanding these properties is crucial for service marketing, as they directly affect how consumers evaluate service offerings and shape marketing strategies to enhance customer perception and satisfaction.

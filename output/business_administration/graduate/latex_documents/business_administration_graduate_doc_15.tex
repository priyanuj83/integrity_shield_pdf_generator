\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item What is the capital of a firm that has total assets of $240,000, total liabilities of $100,000, and an income of \$40,000 for one year?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item \$240,000
    \item \$40,000
    \item \$100,000
    \item \$300,000
    \item \$280,000
\end{enumerate}
\item The salesman for the Fabulous Footwear Co., receive a 1.5\% commission on all sales. What would a salesman's income be for the month in which he sold \$54,600 worth of shoes?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item \$820
    \item \$500
    \item \$730
    \item \$54,600
    \item \$2,190
\end{enumerate}
\item Mr. Williams owns a piece of property assessed at $7,800 in a city which has a tax rate of $2.80 per \$100. How much in property taxes does he pay?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item \$218.40
    \item \$220.00
    \item \$234.00
    \item \$210.00
    \item \$225.60
\end{enumerate}
\item Jacob gave the Franklin Mint a 90-day note for \$1250 on October 10. If it was discounted at the bank on November 15 at 6(1/2)\%, what were the proceeds?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item \$1,200
    \item \$1,215.75
    \item \$1,230.50
    \item \$1,235.00
    \item \$1,242.50
\end{enumerate}
\item What is the effective rate of yield of a stock selling for $60 and paying an annual dividend of $2?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 2(1/2)\%
    \item 3(1/3)\%
    \item 4\%
    \item 5\%
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\item Find the general solution of Bessel's equation of order one.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item y(x) = c\_1 * sin(x) + c\_2 * cos(x)
    \item y(x) = c\_1 * y\_1($x)^2$ + c\_2 * y\_2($x)^2$
    \item y(x) = c\_1 y\_1(x) + c\_2 y\_2(x)
    \item y(x) = c\_1 * x * y\_1(x) + c\_2 / (x * y\_2(x))
    \item y(x) = c\_1 * y\_1(x) / (c\_2 * y\_2(x))
\end{enumerate}
\item Determine the mean-square quantization error during the quantization of a signal. Assume that the error (equally likely) lies in the range - S/2 to + S/2, where S is the step size.

\end{enumerate}

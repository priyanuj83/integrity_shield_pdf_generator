\section*{Multiple Choice (20 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item A change in a neuron membrane potential from +50 millivolts to -70 millivolts is considered
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item depolarization
    \item repolarization
    \item hyperpolarization
    \item an action potential
\end{enumerate}
\item An animal cell that is permeable to water but not salts has an internal NaCl concentration of 10\%. If placed in freshwater the cell will
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item plasmolyze
    \item swell and eventually lyse
    \item endocytose water into a large central vacuole
    \item shrivel
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following processes occur in the cytoplasm of an eukaryotic cell? I. DNA replication II. Transcription III. Translation
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item I only
    \item III only
    \item II and III only
    \item I, II, and III
\end{enumerate}
\item The frequency of a particular allele in a population of 1,000 birds in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium is 0.3. If the population remains in equilibrium, what...
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item It will increase if the allele is favorable or will decrease if it is unfavorable for individuals in that population.
    \item Not enough information is provided to determine if the frequency of the allele will remain the same or change.
    \item The frequency of the allele will remain at 0.3 because the population is at Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium.
    \item The frequency of the allele will remain the same; this is an example of the bottleneck effect.
\end{enumerate}
\item Hawkmoths are insects that are similar in appearance and behavior to hummingbirds. Which of the following is LEAST valid?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item These organisms are examples of convergent evolution.
    \item These organisms were subjected to similar environmental conditions.
    \item These organisms are genetically related to each other.
    \item These organisms have analogous structures.
\end{enumerate}
\item The normal pH of the cytosol is about 7.2. Most enzymes in the lysosome, however, are only active at a pH of 5. This suggests that
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item only acidic substances can undergo enzymatic processing in lysosomes
    \item most enzymes in the lysosome are incapable of catalyzing substrates inside the lysosome
    \item enzymes in the lysosome are released to the cytosol
    \item protons are actively pumped into the lysosome
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following must exist in a population before natural selection can act upon that population?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Genetic variation among individuals
    \item Variation among individuals caused by environmental factors
    \item Sexual reproduction
    \item B and C only
\end{enumerate}
\item Crop rotation is the successive planting of different crops on the same land in sequential seasons. Which of the following statements summarizes the most...
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item It allows the farmer to plant the crops with the greatest demand in the marketplace.
    \item It increases crop yields and soil fertility and minimizes pathogen infection and pest infestation.
    \item It allows the hybridization of successive crops and the development of newer varieties of crop plants.
    \item It decreases the need for diverse harvesting equipment and minimizes production costs.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is not a form of interspecies interaction?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Commensalism
    \item Succession
    \item Mutualism
    \item Parasitism
\end{enumerate}
\item Invertebrate immune systems possess which of the following?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Cytotoxic T-lymphocytes
    \item Phagocytes
    \item B-cells
    \item Helper T-cells
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{True / False (20 marks)}
\textit{Answer True or False and justify briefly.}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'Which of the following is (are) required when performing a polymerase chain reaction (P C R) on a DNA sample?' is 'Reverse transcriptase'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'Imagine an organism whose 2n = 96. Meiosis would leave this organism's cells with how many chromosomes?' is '48'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'A healthy man and woman are worried about having children because they are both carriers of sickle-cell disease. If the couple have a...' is '75\%'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'All of the following are modes of asexual reproduction EXCEPT' is 'meiosis'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'This biome has cold winters and is known for its pine forests.' is 'Deciduous forests'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'To ensure that hatchling sea turtles orient to the sea rather than inland, which of the following is the most environmentally sound...' is 'Building low fences on the sand dunes'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'A new plant was discovered and determined to have an unusually low number of stomata on the undersides of its leaves. For what...' is 'Humid and sunny'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'Which of the following disorders will most likely result from X chromosome aneuploidy in women?' is 'Hemophilia'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'All of the following statements are true EXCEPT' is 'insulin decreases storage of glycogen'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'Which of the following best explains why lignin is important to the evolutionary success of land plants?' is 'Lignin supplies the developing embryo with essential nutrients, enabling its growth.'.
\end{enumerate}

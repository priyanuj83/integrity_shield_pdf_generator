\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following is a biotic factor that could affect the growth rate of a population?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Flash flood
    \item Tsunami
    \item Sudden reduction in the animal food resource
    \item Destruction of the ozone layer
    \item Drought
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following statements about trypsin is NOT true?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item It is an organic compound made of proteins.
    \item It is a catalyst that alters the rate of a reaction.
    \item It is operative over a wide pH range.
    \item The rate of catalysis is affected by the concentration of substrate.
\end{enumerate}
\item Certain nerve gases are known to cause a breakdown of cholinesterase. How would these gases affect the human body and why?

    \item Cause euphoria and hallucinations
    \item Improve memory and cognitive functions
    \item Increase physical strength and endurance
    \item Cause tremors, spasms, or even death
    \item Trigger temporary paralysis without pain
\end{enumerate}
\item Why does milk \textquotedblleftspoil\textquotedblright when kept in a refrigerator?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Enzymatic reactions with oxygen
    \item Lactose crystallization
    \item Loss of refrigeration power over time
    \item mesophiles
    \item Natural separation of milk components without bacterial influence
\end{enumerate}
\item All of the following are examples of events that can prevent interspecific breeding EXCEPT
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the potential mates experience geographic isolation
    \item the potential mates experience behavioral isolation
    \item the potential mates have different courtship rituals
    \item the potential mates have similar breeding seasons
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

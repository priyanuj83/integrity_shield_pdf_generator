\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which nuclide has an NMR frequency of 115.5 MHz in a 20.0 T magnetic field?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 17 O
    \item 19 F
    \item 29 Si
    \item 31 P
\end{enumerate}
\item Cobalt-60 is used in the radiation therapy of cancer and can be produced by bombardment of cobalt-59 with which of the following?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Neutrons
    \item Alpha particles
    \item Beta particles
    \item X-rays
\end{enumerate}
\item The normal modes of a carbon dioxide molecule that are infrared-active include which of the following? I. Bending II. Symmetric stretching III. Asymmetric stretching
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item I only
    \item II only
    \item III only
    \item I and III only
\end{enumerate}
\item Calculate the magnetic field responsible for the polarization of 2.5 x 10^-6 for 13 C at 298 K.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 0.5 T
    \item 1.2 T
    \item 2.9 T
    \item 100 T
\end{enumerate}
\item The T$_{2}$ of an NMR line is 15 ms. Ignoring other sources of linebroadening, calculate its linewidth.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 0.0471 Hz
    \item 21.2 Hz
    \item 42.4 Hz
    \item 66.7 Hz
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\item Which of the following decimal numbers has an exact representation in binary notation?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following comes closest to being a perfectly secure encryption scheme?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

    \item DES (Data Encryption Standard), a symmetric-key algorithm
    \item Enigma, a transposition cipher
    \item One-time pad
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

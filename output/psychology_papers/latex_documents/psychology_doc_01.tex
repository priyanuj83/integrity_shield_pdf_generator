\section*{True / False (20 marks)}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]

\item True or False: The correct answer to 'REM sleep is considered to be paradoxical sleep because' is 'the mind is very active, but the body is in a state of paralysis'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to '"According to Hersey and Blanchard's situational leadership model, a leader should use a ""participative"" style for subordinates who have:"' is 'low ability and high motivation.'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'As a result of an accident, Abdul lost sight in his right eye. To judge the distance of vehicles when he is driving, Abdul is able to...' is 'I only'.

\item True or False: The correct answer to 'Damage to the occipital lobes of the brain would most likely affect which of the senses?' is 'touch'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'In Gestalt therapy, transference is usually' is 'interpreted to the patient early in treatment'.
